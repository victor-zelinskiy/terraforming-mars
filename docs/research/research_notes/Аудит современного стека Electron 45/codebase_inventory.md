## 1. Animation stack: how animations are driven (GSAP, motion-v, Vue Transition, WAAPI, CSS) and the motion-token / hold machinery

### Takeaway
Animation runs on three engines side by side. GSAP 3.15 carries all choreography: 85 files, no plugins, and hand-rolled FLIP/clone flights. Native WAAPI `element.animate` is used about 16 times for simple fades and marker glides. A very large CSS keyframe/transition layer (409 `@keyframes` in source, 412 in the compiled CSS) sits under one speed token, `--motion-scale`. motion-v is a dead dependency. Vue `<transition :css="false">` is the main hook point for handing work to GSAP. A custom "animation hold" registry gates server-driven UI behind animation completion.

### Cited Findings
- **GSAP is the primary engine.** 85 files import `from 'gsap'`, all from the bare `'gsap'` specifier. No plugin is imported anywhere: `Flip.`, `CustomEase`, `gsap.registerPlugin`, `gsap.context`/`matchMedia`, `quickTo`/`quickSetter` and `gsap.utils` all count 0. Call counts: `gsap.set(` 396, `gsap.timeline(` 180, `gsap.killTweensOf` 92, `gsap.to(` 64, `gsap.fromTo(` 46, `gsap.delayedCall` 36, `gsap.ticker` 5 (rg over `src/client`).
- GSAP eases in use, all built-in: `power2.out` 155, `power2.in` 130, `power1.out` 103, `power1.in` 97, `expo.out` 43, plus `back.out(1.5/1.6)`. 66 back/elastic/bounce hits (rg over `ease: '…'` in `src/client`).
- **GSAP ticker bridge.** `src/client/components/motion/gsapMotionBridge.ts:42-47` lazy-imports gsap and sets `gsap.ticker.fps(240 | 60 | 30)` from the player's FPS cap. Lines 22-31 record that a tightened global `lagSmoothing` was tried and REVERTED, because under a starved compositor animations "CRAWL until its own safety timeout snaps it". Flows that must stay continuous drive "their own clock instead: rAF + an interval co-driver + a bounded per-tick step".
- **motion-v is unused.** It is listed in `package.json` dependencies (`"motion-v": "^2.3.0"`), but rg finds zero imports in `src`, `electron`, `tests`, `scripts` or the webpack configs. The only text hit is the HTML attribute `data-motion-variant` in `src/client/components/console/ConsoleRevealOverlay.vue:23`.
- **WAAPI (`element.animate`)** has 16 call sites in 9 files. Examples: the scale marker glide `src/client/components/board/AnimatedScaleMarker.vue:737`; fullscreen-card fades `src/client/components/card/CardZoomModal.vue:873,916`; parliament step fades `src/client/components/console/ConsoleShell.vue:11796,21774,22303`; the chip conveyor `src/client/console/statusChipConveyor.ts:226,280,317`; the surface director `src/client/console/surfaceMotion/surfaceMotionDirector.ts:223`; the draft FLIP `src/client/components/console/draft/ConsoleDraftWorkspace.vue:1233`; lobby screen motion `src/client/console/menu/lobbyScreenMotion.ts:37`. `getAnimations()` is read in 2 places (`src/client/console/cardFlight/landingRect.ts:114-120`, `src/client/console/cursorArrival.ts:160-163`).
- **CSS layer.** 409 `@keyframes`, 626 `transition:`/`transition+:` declarations, 476 `animation:` declarations and 1,094 `var(--motion-scale` usages across `src/styles` + `src/client`. Compiled `build/styles.css` has 412 `@keyframes` and 95 `infinite`. `docs/PERFORMANCE_AUDIT.md` (Iteration 4) classifies 115 real `infinite` declarations: 96 functional, 12 ambient-element, 7 ambient-fullbleed.
- **Vue transitions.** `<Transition>` (capitalised) appears 19 times in 17 files and lowercase `<transition>` 149 times in 56 files (39 of them in `ConsoleShell.vue`). `<TransitionGroup>`/`<transition-group>` appears 5 + 10 times. `:css="false"` (JS-hook transitions) 46 times, and `@enter`/`@leave`/`@after-*` hooks 59 times. The surface-motion director is "a pair of Vue `<transition :css="false">` hooks the shell binds to every MIGRATED band surface" and plays "transform/opacity ONLY" GSAP choreography (`src/client/console/surfaceMotion/surfaceMotionDirector.ts:1-31`). The markup contract `data-motion-surface` appears 38 times in `.vue` files.
- **Motion tokens.** The single source is `src/client/components/motion/motionTokens.ts`. Speed presets `calm 1.3 / standard 1 / swift 0.65` (lines 35-43) come from `?motion=` or localStorage `tm_motion_speed`; a numeric `?motion=0.5` override is also supported (lines ~125-140). `motionMs(base)` sits at line 147 and has 591 calls in 128 files. `applyMotionCssScale()` writes `--motion-scale` on `<html>` (lines 157-162). `MOTION_EASE` (lines 53-64) holds five `cubic-bezier` strings: standard, enter, exit, `settle` "Damped-spring style" `cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.26, 0.44, 1)`, and convey. `CURSOR_MS = 180` sits at line 73. `createFrameGate()` (lines 241-259) throttles rAF loops for a 30/60 fps cap. Lines 25-27 document the limitation: "CSS/WAAPI animations are composited by the browser and are not throttleable from here".
- **Custom easing code.** `src/client/components/motion/cubicBezier.ts` is a Newton-Raphson + bisection `cubic-bezier()` solver ("so a JS tween can ride the SAME curve a CSS transition declares"). It has exactly 1 consumer file. No spring physics was found: rg for `stiffness|damping|spring(|lerp(` in client `.ts` returns nothing. "Spring" is only a bezier with overshoot.
- **Animation hold registry.** `src/client/components/presentation/animationHold.ts` (531 lines): `registerAnimationHoldSupplier` (line 223), `beginAnimationHold` (294), `holdAnimationWhile` (317), `holdForGsapAnimation` (341), `DEFAULT_MAX_HOLD_MS = 35_000` (101). Contract (`.claude/rules/animations.md:20-29`): a supplier predicate must read Vue-reactive state, the release is the flow's own completion signal (GSAP `onComplete`/`onInterrupt`, "NEVER a `setTimeout`"), and a 1 s watchdog sweep reconciles phantoms. `presentationFlow.ts` (362 lines) adds the single-foreground lease/FIFO for notifications.
- **FLIP / card flights (hand-rolled, no GSAP Flip).** `src/client/console/cardFlight/cloneFlights.ts:1-22,73-` builds a `position:fixed` layer appended to `document.body` (line ~75, `z-index:11640`). Each clone gets its own CSS `zoom` (= rendered rect / natural width) and moves only by a delta. Touchdown = "the real slot materializes UNDER the clone, the clone leaves on the NEXT frame". The landing target comes from `restingRectOf(el)` (`src/client/console/cardFlight/landingRect.ts:207`). That helper subtracts inline GSAP translations and "un-maps" live CSS animations/transitions on the element and its ancestors to their end keyframe (via `getAnimations` + `DOMMatrixReadOnly`, lines 1-40,114-120). The 3D card body is `src/client/console/cardFlight/card3dInner.ts:1-40` (`perspective` on the outer element, `preserve-3d` + `backface-visibility` on the inner, "NEVER animate `opacity` on the inner").
- **Resource-token flights.** `src/client/console/resourceTransfer/resourceTransferDirector.ts:1-16`: "the pop out of the source → ONE progress tween mapping the whole flight (position + … scale + … tilt) through the model's arc → the two-frame touchdown settle → the contact beat … → the absorb". The arcs are hand-computed quadratics with a viewport ceiling (`resourceTransferModel.ts:348-375`, `transferArcPlan`), re-targeted on the final approach (`TRANSFER_RETARGET_AT`, `transferRetargetWeight` line 122). `bornThenFlown` (line 121) makes the chip visible on one GSAP tick and builds the timeline on the next, because "GSAP's global time advances ONLY at a tick" (`.claude/rules/animations.md:45-46`). The family is about 3.2k lines in `src/client/console/resourceTransfer/*.ts` (consoleResourceTransfer 1,299 · railReward 632 · director 547 · model 724 · transferEndVisible 147).
- **Forced-reflow animation restarts.** `void el.offsetWidth` replays a CSS one-shot in 10 places, e.g. `src/client/console/boardPeek.ts:112`, `src/client/console/revealSourcePeek.ts:30`, `src/client/components/cardAnnotations/CardAnnotationsLayer.vue:642`, `src/client/console/parliament/sittingDirector.ts:555`.

### Inferences
- GSAP is used purely as a tween/timeline clock plus transform setter (about 400 `gsap.set` calls). Every FLIP is re-implemented per director: measure → fixed-layer clone → delta tween → next-frame handoff. That makes the code a candidate for (a) native WAAPI with `composite`/`commitStyles` for simple fades, (b) same-document View Transitions for the "one surface advances" workspace phase changes now done with `<transition :css="false">` + GSAP, and (c) CSS `linear()` easing in place of the JS cubic solver and the "settle" overshoot bezier. Whether these apply depends on Chromium 156 behaviour inside nested `zoom` contexts, which other researchers must verify.
- motion-v can be removed from `package.json` with no code change.
- The `void offsetWidth` restarts are candidates for `el.getAnimations().forEach(a => { a.currentTime = 0; a.play(); })` or WAAPI one-shots.
- Much of the hold-registry complexity exists because GSAP `onComplete` does not fire on kill, so every await must also chain `onInterrupt`. WAAPI's `Animation.finished` promise rejects on cancel, which is a natively settling signal (inference).

### Gaps
- No exact count of how many GSAP timelines are FLIP-style vs. simple fades. That would need per-director reading.
- No measurement of GSAP ticker cost vs. compositor-driven WAAPI on the target hardware was found in the docs.

## 2. DOM moves: `<Teleport>` "embedded stages", remount/state-loss workarounds, `moveBefore`

### Takeaway
"Embedded stages" are built from Vue `<Teleport :to="selector ?? 'body'" :disabled="…">`, which re-parents ONE live component instance into a zone published by the host. A selector-registry directive (`v-embed-zone`) guards against teleporting into nodes that don't exist yet. `moveBefore` is not used anywhere. Re-parenting side effects and v-show restarts of CSS animations are documented pain points.

### Cited Findings
- `<Teleport` appears 55 times in 44 files. `ConsoleShell.vue` alone has 9, all with the same shape, e.g. `src/client/components/console/ConsoleShell.vue:207` `<Teleport :to="handEmbedTarget ?? 'body'" :disabled="handEmbedTarget === undefined">`, plus 259, 367, 692, 710, 748, 789, 908 and 1513 (hand / colonies / parliament / external draw / task host / effect decision / deck pick / reveal / play).
- Hosts publish zones as attribute selectors, e.g. `data-embed-slot="action-colonies"`, `"workspace-reveal"`, `"action-parliament"`, `"action-hand"` (`src/client/components/console/ConsoleActionComposer.vue:105,132,780,790,791`). The frame slot is set imperatively: `setWorkspaceFrameSlot('card-actions', '[data-embed-slot="action-colonies"]')` (`ConsoleActionComposer.vue:3086,3307`; `ConsoleCardActions.vue:1645`).
- `src/client/console/embedZoneRegistry.ts:1-60`: "a published selector outlives its node in four ordinary ways … Vue resolves the target to nothing, leaves the content in the shell's own flow, and never looks again while the string stays the same — a decision mounted, pickable and invisible". The fix is a `v-embed-zone` directive that keeps a Set of mounted elements and a reactive epoch (`embedZoneMounted(selector)`).
- `src/client/console/consoleWorkspaceStack.ts` (1,863 lines) is "the ONE model of «how deep am I, and inside what»". Its header (lines 1-60) recounts a soft-lock in which a teleported colonies picker "rendered nowhere" after its host unmounted.
- Teleport re-homing preserves the instance: "`<Teleport>` re-targets, `mounted()` never re-runs" (`src/client/components/console/ConsoleRevealOverlay.vue:1774`). `docs/claude/console/workspace-embed.md:84` says a second copy instead of a teleport "forks input and loses" state.
- **v-show restart class.** The console board is `v-show` (hidden while a section workspace is open), and "`display:none → ''` … BY SPEC restarts all CSS animations in the subtree" (memory note `vshow-board-restarts-css-animations.md`; fix in `BoardSpaceTile.vue` with guard `tests/client/components/board/BoardSpaceTile.spec.ts`). Also: "`v-show` FIRES the enter/leave pair" (`.claude/rules/console-ui.md:291`).
- **GSAP re-parenting.** "`gsap._getMatrix` considers a target with `offsetParent === null && rect.width === 0` detached and TEMPORARILY moves it into `<html>` to measure … 49 proxies = 98 root mutations per spawn". Fixed by giving the fixed proxy chassis an intrinsic size (`docs/PLAYED_TABLEAU_PERFORMANCE.md:12-21`).
- `moveBefore` has 0 occurrences in `src/client`, `src/styles` and `assets`.
- The historical remount model (`<player-home :key="playerkey">` remounted on every server response) was replaced by structural sharing (`viewSnapshotShare.ts`). The `?remount=1` flag was deleted 2026-08-23 (`docs/REMOUNT_ANIMATION_REWORK_DESIGN.md:1-30`).

### Inferences
- Vue 3.5's Teleport moves nodes with `insertBefore`. Chromium's atomic `Node.prototype.moveBefore()` (state-preserving: animations, focus, iframes, transitions) would directly target the "teleport into a zone" pattern, but only if Vue or a custom directive used it. No such shim exists today. Availability in Chromium 156 is for the platform researchers to confirm.
- The `v-show` → `display:none` restart could be replaced by `content-visibility: hidden` (keeps rendering state and doesn't restart animations), with the caveat that it would hide the subtree from the board SelectSpace handlers. Inference to verify. `content-visibility` is currently 0 uses, "deferred" because it "corrupts the fit-loop `scrollHeight`" (`docs/PERFORMANCE_AUDIT.md`, "Still the frontier").

### Gaps
- I did not find a doc that measured the cost of Teleport re-parenting itself (style/layout invalidation on move).

## 3. Positioning and measurement: tooltips, popovers, observers, rect loops, rAF loops, text-fit

### Takeaway
All anchored UI is positioned by hand. The one tooltip primitive is a pure-CSS `::after` fixed above its host, with no collision handling. Popovers are `<Teleport to="body">` + `getBoundingClientRect` + manual flip/clamp math. The codebase leans heavily on synchronous geometry reads: 344 `getBoundingClientRect` in 135 files, 130 `offsetWidth/Height`, 97 `getComputedStyle` in 52 files, 127 `ResizeObserver` mentions in 39 files, and 168 `requestAnimationFrame` in 67 files. Text-fit is a deterministic character-count tier ladder, plus a DOM step-down pass, plus one container-query-unit formula.

### Cited Findings
- **Tooltip.** The mixin is `.premium-tooltip(@max-width: 240px)` at `src/styles/card_action_buttons.less:55-82`: `content: attr(data-hint); position: absolute; bottom: calc(100% + 6px); left: 50%; transform: translateX(-50%)`, shown on `:hover` or the gamepad class `.gp-focus`. It has 17 call sites. `data-hint` appears 28 times in `src/client`. The native `title` attribute is banned in new UI (CLAUDE.md invariant 5), yet 21 `title=`/`:title=` remain in `components/console` (rg; some may be component props).
- **JS-positioned popovers.** `src/client/components/journal/CardPreviewPopover.vue:2,28-37,71-106` uses `<Teleport to="body">`, reads `getBoundingClientRect()`, `window.innerWidth/innerHeight`, "flip to the right", "clamp into the viewport", and writes `left/top` px. `src/client/components/board/BoardCellInfoPopover.vue:259-275,300` uses `getBoundingClientRect`, a manual above/below choice, `position: fixed`. `src/client/components/board/scaleTooltipState.ts:108-210` holds a pure `clampScaleTooltipPosition` with safe zones, and `ScaleTooltip.vue:76-96` feeds it `innerWidth/innerHeight`. There are 13 `*Popover*/*Tooltip*` components (journal ×5, board ×4, hand, notifications, additionalResources).
- **Native popover API.** `popover=`, `showPopover`, `togglePopover`, `:popover-open` and `popovertarget` all count 0. The 182 "popover" hits are component names.
- **CSS anchor positioning.** `anchor-name`, `position-anchor`, `position-area`, `position-try` all count 0 (the 8 `anchor(` hits are a JS method in `ConsoleHandRevealLayer.vue`).
- **Observers.** `ResizeObserver` has 127 mentions in 39 files (top: `ConsoleBoardSection.vue` 10, `foundation/ConsoleScrollArea.vue` 6, `board/AnimatedScaleMarker.vue` 5, `console/composables/useWorkspaceBandGeometry.ts` 4); many go through VueUse `useResizeObserver`. `IntersectionObserver` has 0. `MutationObserver` has 8 mentions in 2 files. The key one is `src/client/console/conWsPresenceBridge.ts:1-30,134`, which replaced root-anchored `:has()` selectors.
- **Layout-read hot spots.** `getBoundingClientRect` top files: `ConsoleBoardSection.vue` 21, `ConsoleStartScene.vue` 12, `console/consoleActionCommitMotion.ts` 9, `ConsoleEndgameWorkspace.vue` 9, `played/ConsolePlayedCategoryView.vue` 8. `scrollHeight/scrollWidth` appear 28 times in 16 files. The board reticle uses a Vue computed over `getBoundingClientRect` that has to subscribe to a "geometry epoch" (`.claude/rules/console-ui.md:111`).
- **Effective-zoom math.** `src/client/console/cssUnits.ts:32-39` `effectiveZoom(el)` = `getBoundingClientRect().width / offsetWidth` (a forced layout per call). There are 18 `effZoom/effectiveZoom` refs. "FLIP transforms inside a CSS `zoom:` context need `effZoom` compensation" (`.claude/rules/console-ui.md:289`). `currentCSSZoom` has 0 uses.
- **rAF.** `requestAnimationFrame` appears 168 times in 67 files (top: `ConsoleStartScene.vue` 14, `ConsoleShell.vue` 7, `console/consoleZoomMotion.ts` 6). rAF stops on a quiet screen, so probes use `src/client/console/probeTick.ts:1-46`, "requestAnimationFrame WITH A TIMER FALLBACK". The cause: a probe waiting on rAF held a mandatory surface for "30 s" (Pluto payout, about 25 % of e2e runs).
- **Fit engines (layout thrash class).** `src/client/console/` holds `cardStripFit.ts`, `consoleActionCanvasFit.ts`, `consoleAlbumLayout.ts`, `consoleDetailFit.ts`, `consoleWsStageLayout.ts` (the "gap = focusOverhang(zoom) + glowPad" solver, lines 1-30). `docs/PERFORMANCE_AUDIT.md` OV-3 records an interleaved "write-`--*-zoom`(CSS `zoom`)→read-`scrollHeight`" loop of up to 8× and 14× iterations, i.e. a "forced full-subtree reflow per iteration".
- **Text fit / length tiers.** `src/client/components/premiumCard/titleFit.ts:1-60` maps the translated title to a 4-tier character-count ladder (≤14/22/34/…) and a prose tier (≤90/140/…). The last mile is CSS: "shrinks the type until the LONGEST WORD fits the container's real inline size (`100cqi` against `--pcard-title-longest`)" (`src/styles/premium_card.less:398,409,470`). `src/client/console/descTier.ts:1-40,109-` runs `fitDescTiers()`, a DOM post-layout step-down (brief → regular → dense) because "A length ladder cannot know its host's width". `src/client/console/consoleTextMeasure.ts:1-40` measures label widths with canvas `measureText` + `getComputedStyle` font for the command-bar fit plan.
- **Scroll.** `ConsoleScrollArea.vue:1-40` is the only scroll container: native scrollbar hidden, custom rail, `ensureVisible(el)` / `scrollByPx` driven by the pad, `useResizeObserver` on viewport + content. "a native scrollbar is a BUG" (`.claude/rules/console-ui.md:268-270`). `scroll-snap`, `scroll-timeline` and `animation-timeline` count 0. `scrollIntoView` has 21 uses in 17 files.

### Inferences
- The `.premium-tooltip` mixin, the 13 hand-positioned popovers and `scaleTooltipState`'s clamp are direct candidates for CSS anchor positioning (`anchor-name` / `position-anchor` / `position-area` / `position-try-fallbacks`) plus the `popover` attribute (top layer, light dismiss). That would remove the `<Teleport to="body">` + `getBoundingClientRect` + `innerWidth` clamp code. One caveat: the console's d-pad focus model uses `.gp-focus`, not `:hover`, so `interestfor`/hint popovers would still need class-driven toggling. Inference.
- `effectiveZoom()` (forced layout) → `Element.currentCSSZoom` (inference; verify availability in 156).
- `fitDescTiers`, title tiers and `consoleTextMeasure` could partly move to `text-wrap: balance/pretty` (already used 6×) and container query units/`@container` style queries. The "no cut word" requirement still needs measurement, though. Inference.
- The rAF-starvation workaround (`probeTick`) and timer-backed probes could use `scheduler.yield()`/`postTask` (0 uses today) for "has anything happened" waits. Inference.

### Gaps
- I could not quantify how many popovers are still reachable in the console product vs. frozen desktop code (`docs/DESKTOP_DEPRECATION_AUDIT.md` would decide). Journal popovers are SHARED per CLAUDE.md.
- No profile isolating the cost of the `getBoundingClientRect` loops at 4K was found beyond the OV-3/BRD-3 notes.

## 5. Visual effects: glass, glow, borders, particles, planet/board, card art, fonts, canvas/WebGL, chart.js

### Takeaway
Console "glass" is faked with flat radial/linear gradients and box-shadow (2,159 `box-shadow`, 691 gradients in console*.less). There is no live blur, and every `filter`/`text-shadow` is force-disabled in console by a permanent paint baseline. Borders are clip-path polygons. The board and planets are DOM + webp sprites. Charts are hand-built SVG, except one lazy chart.js chart. There is no canvas FX, WebGL or particle engine; a WebGPU ambient layer was investigated and rejected.

### Cited Findings
- **Paint baseline.** `src/styles/console_paint_baseline.less:37-39` sets `html.console-native * { filter: none !important; text-shadow: none !important; }`. Per `.claude/rules/console-ui.md:281-285`, "kills `filter` and `text-shadow` unconditionally … there is no toggle and none may return. `box-shadow` is deliberately KEPT", and functional cues must use `box-shadow`/`outline`/colour. So the 283 `drop-shadow` / 497 `filter:` hits are dead in console (shared/desktop residue).
- **Glass/dim without blur.** `src/styles/console.less:347-376`: `.con-backdrop-dim()` / `.con-backdrop-dim-strong()` are radial gradients — "PERF (Steam Deck): premium full-screen DIM without backdrop-filter blur … the single most GPU-expensive effect on this hardware … never reintroduce backdrop-filter here". One always-mounted `.con-shade` fades opacity behind every migrated surface (`console.less:378-`).
- **Ambient loops.** "An INFINITE (ambient) loop must be compositor-only: opacity/transform … never box-shadow". Three box-shadow loops kept "a full style→Layerize→Paint pipeline running ~58×/s on an idle board (~55 % of a ×4-throttled main thread)". The fix pattern is a static shadow plus an `::after` overlay that breathes on opacity (`.claude/rules/console-ui.md:286`; memory `steam-deck-perf-iteration-3.md`).
- **Borders.** `@cab-shape: polygon(8px 0, 100% 0, calc(100% - 8px) 100%, 0 100%)` and "clip-path replaces border-radius/overflow" (`src/styles/card_action_buttons.less:94,~104`). `clip-path` appears 47 times in console*.less and 153 overall. `corner-shape` has 0 uses.
- **Focus ring.** `.con-cursor()` mixin (`src/styles/console.less:253-330`): the ring is a pseudo-element layer whose `opacity` animates (compositor-only); the lift is `transform: scale(calc(1 + .035 * var(--con-cursor-on)))` on one clock (`--con-cursor-ms` 180 ms). The doc gives the reason: "shadow lists that cannot interpolate … a transition between lists of different inset-ness is discrete" (`docs/claude/console/cursor.md`, "Why").
- **3D.** `perspective` 24 and `preserve-3d` 10 in console*.less (card turns, `card3dInner.ts`).
- **Planet art.** `src/client/components/console/ConsolePlanetDisc.vue:1-40` uses `assets/colonies-planets/<key>.webp` (1024² alpha discs) as `background-size: cover` in a `border-radius: 50%` box, with sheen/terminator/rim painted as CSS layers.
- **Board.** The board is DOM. Runtime textures were converted to webp: `board/mars.png` 4.64→0.32 MB, `resources/card.png` 2.94→0.36 MB, `board_icons_ares` 2.73→1.98 MB lossless. The doc adds a caveat: webp does not cut "the GPU raster of the DECODED bitmap (dimensions unchanged)" (`docs/PERFORMANCE_AUDIT.md`, "WebP (BND-3)"). `PlayerCube` renders "~30 SVG nodes … per owned cell" (BRD-4). `docs/STEAM_DECK_PERF_ITERATION_3` (via memory) found "0 `<img>`, 0MB decoded" on the board.
- **Card art.** `scripts/make-card-art-thumbs.mjs:1-30`: full art `assets/card-images/<key>.webp` is 1536×1024 (~284 KiB, ~6 MiB decoded); thumbs `thumb/<key>.webp` are 512×341 (~20-40 KiB, ~0.7 MiB decoded), "9× less decode CPU". Dense surfaces use thumbs, fullscreen uses full. `assets/` holds 1,246 `.webp`, 1,222 `.png` and 16 `.jpg`, about 956 MB on disk (`du`). The tableau uses "staged mount (hydration)": faces arrive in waves of ~36 per rAF tick (`docs/PLAYED_TABLEAU_PERFORMANCE.md:35-40`).
- **Boot GPU warm-up workaround.** `src/client/components/boot/AppBootLoader.vue:15-70` pre-renders cards, board spaces, cubes, arc scales and backdrop/blur/glow cells in an invisible `inert` subtree. It also opens a near-invisible top-layer `<dialog>` via `showModal()`, because "The FIRST top-layer surface + transformed stage of a session hits a Skia Graphite first-allocation race on Windows ('ProduceSkia: non-existent mailbox')". Memory `e2e-gpu-cold-shader-cache-stall.md` records first-draw shader compile stalls of 64-103 ms on fresh profiles.
- **Canvas/WebGL/charts.** There is no WebGL/WebGPU. chart.js is used ONLY by `src/client/components/gameend/VictoryPointChart.vue:11-19` (dynamic `import('chart.js')` into the `chartjs` async chunk, 197 KB). Its consumer is `GameEnd.vue:239`, loaded via `defineAsyncComponent` from `App.vue:268`. The console's endgame charts are hand-built SVG: `src/client/components/console/ConsoleOvChart.vue:1-12` ("Built for a 4K TV read from a couch and a D-PAD … this SVG carries geometry") and `components/endgame/EndgameLineChart.vue`.
- **Particles/FX.** "An ambient WebGPU/canvas FX layer was investigated and REJECTED — the premise did not survive counting": only 7 full-bleed decorative infinite animations exist, 1 of them paint-heavy, all on once-per-game screens (`docs/PERFORMANCE_AUDIT.md`, Iteration 4).
- **`mix-blend-mode`** 7 and **`mask-image`/`-webkit-mask`** 14 in console*.less.

### Inferences
- Because filters are banned in console, effects that today need gradients plus extra pseudo-elements (glows, rims, glass) are candidates for cheaper or more expressive primitives: `corner-shape` for the parallelogram/bevel clip-path chrome (it keeps box-shadow, which clip-path clips), `@property`-typed animatable gradients/colours (only 1 today), and `color-mix()`/relative colour in console tokens. Inference; perf parity must be checked on Deck/Graphite.
- The boot warm-up is a GPU-driver workaround tied to Skia Graphite on Windows. Its continued need on Chromium 156 should be re-tested rather than assumed.

### Gaps
- The visual cost of `clip-path` on box-shadowed chrome (clip-path clips the outer shadow) was not documented. I couldn't confirm whether designers work around this with wrapper elements.
- I couldn't determine VRAM residency for card art at 4K from the docs.

## 7. Gamepad and input: architecture, Linux native bridge, focus/cursor model, rumble

### Takeaway
Input is the W3C Gamepad API, polled by an idle-adaptive "rAF + setInterval" double driver. A pure poll model emits semantic intents through a single-slot router into console screens; keyboard is synthesized into the same stream. On Linux/Steam Deck an Electron main-process joydev reader substitutes for the broken Chromium fetcher. Focus is per-surface CSS ("the cursor is one living object"), with a pure spatial-navigation scorer for the generic focus engine. Rumble/haptics is designed but NOT built.

### Cited Findings
- **Modules.** `src/client/gamepad/` (3,238 lines): `gamepadCore.ts` (DOM shell, poll loop), `gamepadPollModel.ts` (pure), `focusEngine.ts` (704 lines), `focusScopes.ts`, `spatialNav.ts`, `glyphSets.ts`, `glyphCssBridge.ts`, `nativePadBridge.ts` (145 lines), `inputModeState.ts`, `buttonLayout.ts`, `hintModel.ts`, `gamepadSettings.ts`. UI components live in `src/client/components/gamepad/` (`GamepadLayer.vue` 26 KB, `GamepadFocusRing.vue`, `GamepadGlyph.vue`, `GamepadHintBar.vue`).
- **Poll loop.** `src/client/gamepad/gamepadCore.ts:10-23`: "The Gamepad API does NOT buffer input … With an rAF-only loop, any frame long enough to span a tap … silently EATS that press … NEVER reduce this back to rAF-only". Idle-adaptive drivers (lines 25-38): `POLL_INTERVAL_HOT_MS = 8` (line 105) + rAF while hot, `POLL_INTERVAL_REST_MS = 16` (line 109) timer-only at rest. The reason given: "a permanently re-arming rAF forces the renderer to produce frames at display rate forever … every `getGamepads()` call allocates a fresh pad/button object graph (~185 polls/s ⇒ constant minor-GC pressure at rest)". The timer is created at `gamepadCore.ts:232`.
- **Linux native bridge.** `electron/nativeGamepadLinux.ts:1-33`: on Steam Deck the renderer logged "`pads=0`" in every session while the kernel showed three live joysticks. "Chromium's Linux fetcher only accepts devices carrying the udev property `ID_INPUT_JOYSTICK` and keys device identity on the syspath up to the first 'input' — which for every uinput device is the SAME string, `/sys/devices/virtual/`. Steam Input publishes its pads exactly there." The bridge reads `/dev/input/js*` (`struct js_event`, 8 bytes, lines 44-50) and pushes "ready-made W3C standard-mapping snapshots" over IPC `desktop:native-pads` every 8 ms (lines 53-60). It also "sidesteps Chromium's 4-pad cap and its user-gesture visibility gate", and is used ONLY while `getGamepads()` reports no pads (`src/client/gamepad/nativePadBridge.ts`). Steam Input mirroring is handled by the renderer's single-driver election (lines 29-33; `docs/GAMEPAD_SUPPORT_DESIGN.md` §8.1-8.3).
- **Semantic input.** `src/client/console/composables/useConsoleInput.ts:1-45`: screens claim "the single console intent slot" and receive `onAction('primary'|'back'|'inspect'…)` / `onNav(dir, repeat)`. Keyboard is synthesized by `consoleKeyBridge.ts` (155 lines, VueUse `useEventListener`). Unhandled intents may fall through "to the demoted DOM focus engine".
- **Spatial nav.** `src/client/gamepad/spatialNav.ts:1-30` scores directional candidates purely from rects (`ORTHO_WEIGHT = 2.2`, `ALIGNED_ORTHO_WEIGHT = 0.3`, `CONE_RATIO = 2.5`) and is tuned for hex boards.
- **Cursor model (console).** "THE CURSOR IS ONE LIVING OBJECT — it ARRIVES, it never appears". One clock `--con-cursor-ms`/`CURSOR_MS` (180 ms × motion scale), ring = pseudo-element opacity, lift = host transform. A default focus "waits for its surface's ready edge (`cursorArrival.ts`)" (`docs/claude/console/cursor.md`; `.claude/rules/animations.md:59-60`). A separate generic `GamepadFocusRing.vue` overlay (positioned via `:style="ringStyle"`) serves the non-console focus engine. Hover parity: the tooltip mixin also shows on `.gp-focus` (`src/styles/card_action_buttons.less:58-61`).
- **Glyph abstraction.** Button names exist only in `glyphSets.ts`; markup uses `GamepadGlyph.vue`; CSS reads `var(--gp-label-<control>)` from `glyphCssBridge.ts` (CLAUDE.md invariant 12).
- **Rumble/haptics: not implemented.** `vibrationActuator`, `navigator.vibrate` and `dual-rumble` have 0 code hits (the 7 `playEffect` hits are `playEffectsDetailStep` etc.). The design calls for "`haptics.ts` wrapper over `vibrationActuator.playEffect('dual-rumble', …)`" with status "Phase 5 — PENDING" (`docs/GAMEPAD_SUPPORT_DESIGN.md:380-382,596`). `docs/CONSOLE_MODE_CONCEPT.md:538` lists "Haptics (planned, unbuilt)".
- **DevTools pad cursor.** `electron/devtoolsPadCursor.ts:1-15` runs a main-process ~60 Hz `executeJavaScript` loop that draws a virtual mouse in the DevTools frontend for Deck/Steam Machine.
- **E2E artefact.** A physical Steam Controller on the Steam Machine leaks into headless Chromium through `/dev`, so the harness stubs `navigator.getGamepads` (recent commit 6c396268a3; memory `e2e-gamepad-reaches-headless-browser.md`).

### Inferences
- Polling at 8-16 ms with `getGamepads()` allocations is inherent to the W3C Gamepad API; Chromium has no event-driven button API. The native bridge already proves an IPC push model works. Inference: the Linux bridge could become the primary source on Linux if latency is equal.
- Haptics would be a greenfield use of `GamepadHapticActuator.playEffect('dual-rumble' | 'trigger-rumble')`. Trigger-rumble support depends on platform/controller, for the platform researchers to verify.
- Spatial nav is hand-rolled. Inference: the CSS/JS spatial navigation proposals are not shipping in Chromium, so this likely stays (verify).

### Gaps
- No documented input-latency measurements (press → first painted beat) for Windows vs. Deck were found, beyond "Worst-case added latency on the FIRST press after 1.5 s of quiet: 8 ms" (`gamepadCore.ts:37`).

## 9. Performance pain points (4K TV / Steam Deck / Windows): what is slow and what was done

### Takeaway
The bottleneck is main-thread RENDERING: style recalc (`UpdateLayoutTree`), Layerize and Paint. JavaScript is not. It is driven by a 22k-rule global stylesheet, heavy card DOM, CSS `zoom`-based scaling with read/write fit loops, and invalidation-heavy selectors. The fixes so far are cull-and-ban rules: no `filter`/`text-shadow`/`backdrop-filter`, no root `:has()`, no universal sibling selectors, compositor-only ambient loops, idle-adaptive polling and staged mounts. Some 4K numbers from e2e were SwiftShader artefacts.

### Cited Findings
- **Trace 1 (2026-07-02, 264 s of play).** `UpdateLayoutTree` 8,140 ms, `Layerize` 7,991 ms, `Commit` 3,279 ms, Paint+PrePaint ~3,100 ms, vs. JS `FunctionCall` 867 ms. "The remaining jank is RENDERING/COMPOSITING-bound, NOT JavaScript". After the backdrop cull, Layerize was 4,196 ms (−48 %) and UpdateLayoutTree ~8,269 ms "now the clear #1". "UpdateLayoutTree … card-mount style recalc … need reducing the number of cards styled/mounted at once (windowing/virtualization)" (`docs/PERFORMANCE_AUDIT.md`, "Trace findings", "Iteration 2").
- **Root `:has()`.** A full-document recalc per animated frame, ~9,400 elements per pass. The bridge took UpdateLayoutTree from 4,994 ms to 213 ms per episode. A separate GSAP `_getMatrix` re-parent caused "98 root mutations per spawn" (`docs/PLAYED_TABLEAU_PERFORMANCE.md:12-33`).
- **Universal sibling selector.** One `.x__tokens > * + *` rule pushed style recalc for a mount from 323 to 1,204 elements (2.9 → 7.0 ms). There is now a guard `tests/console/universalSiblingSelectorGuard.spec.ts` (memory `owl-selector-universal-sibling-invalidation.md`).
- **Steam Deck iteration 3.** On an idle board, "Layerize 805ms + UpdateLayoutTree 429ms + Paint 360ms per 3 s", with the full pipeline running ~58×/s from three box-shadow loops. The fix was opacity-breathing pseudo-elements. Other fixes: gamepad idle-adaptive polling; the snapshot share walking `toRaw(prev)` instead of the live proxy; identity-stable MA HUD zones; the presence-bridge record filter (memory `steam-deck-perf-iteration-3.md`; full report `docs/STEAM_DECK_PERF_ITERATION_3.md`).
- **Steam Deck iteration 1.** The action wheel's first open went from 413-466 to 181-306 ms (deck profile 1280×800, CPU×4 proxy). The leak detector moved from ~30-38 document traversals per second to one `querySelectorAll` (memory `steam-deck-perf-iteration-1.md`). The leak detector still ticks every 1 s (`src/client/console/consoleLeakDetector.ts:562`).
- **Windows paradox.** "the Electron client is noticeably SMOOTHER on a Steam Deck … than on a much more powerful Windows laptop". Suspected causes: NVIDIA packaged-exe throttle, DWM/MPO flip-model thrash, wrong-GPU/hybrid pacing (`docs/PERFORMANCE_AUDIT.md`, Iteration 3). Measurement tooling exists: `scripts/capture-presentmon.ps1`, `scripts/analyze-presentmon.cjs` (Iteration 4).
- **SwiftShader artefact.** "PL-149 «4K commit stall 150–210 ms» was SwiftShader". On the RTX 4070 D3D11 path, commit waits were 110-140 ms total vs. 1.25-1.42 s in software (memory `perf-measure-on-gpu-not-swiftshader.md`). The first draw on a fresh profile stalls 64-103 ms for shader compile (memory `e2e-gpu-cold-shader-cache-stall.md`).
- **CSS `zoom`.** `zoom:` appears in 115 Less declarations (256 in compiled CSS) plus 95 JS/inline uses. "Embedded px content integrates via `zoom:` multiplied by `var(--con-ui-scale, 1)`" (`.claude/rules/console-ui.md:279`). The TV scale model multiplies the rem base by `--con-ui-scale` ("no root transform / no browser zoom") (`src/styles/console_tv.less:10-48`). `computeTvUiScale` = min(w/1920, h/1080) (`src/client/console/consoleLayoutProfile.ts:163-167`). "B8 — `zoom:` instead of `transform: scale`" and OV-3 (zoom write/scrollHeight read loops) are documented debts (`docs/PERF_INVESTIGATION.md` B8; `docs/PERFORMANCE_AUDIT.md` OV-3). `console-perf-mode-contract.md` (memory) notes "~62% of frame during CSS-`zoom` GSAP-FLIP overlay animations" in style/layout/Layerize.
- **Stylesheet weight.** `build/styles.css` is 4.6 MB raw (778 KB br, 1.11 MB gz). Comments alone are 1.5 MB, 34 %: the file is 4,547,514 chars, 3,002,867 without comments. There are about 21,965 rule blocks. It is loaded render-blocking (`assets/index.html:24`), compiled by `lessc` without minification (`package.json` `make:css`).
- **JS bundle.** `build/main.js` 2.58 MB, `build/vendors.js` 387 KB, the lazy `chunks/console-shell.js` 3.0 MB (the shell chunk), `chunks/console-menu.js` 233 KB, `chunks/chartjs.js` 197 KB (shared build dir, 2026-10-21).
- **Open items.** NotificationLayer fetch+diff per view swap (A1, partly mitigated by a signature skip); `liveCardResources` Map rebuild (A2); `card-hover-tall` height animation (CARD-3); OV-2 ResizeObserver self-trigger; `contain`/`content-visibility` deferred ("`content-visibility` corrupts the fit-loop `scrollHeight`; `contain: paint` clips card badges") (`docs/PERFORMANCE_AUDIT.md`, backlog + "Still the frontier").

### Inferences
- The top remaining cost (style recalc on mounting many cards; global-stylesheet breadth) is the area where `@scope`, real `@layer`, `content-visibility: auto` with `contain-intrinsic-size`, and leaner per-component CSS could matter more than any GPU flag. This is an inference for the report writer to weigh against the documented `content-visibility`/`contain` regressions.
- Stripping comments and minifying `styles.css` (`lessc --clean-css` or a postcss step) is a mechanical win for parse time; it does not change the selector count.
- The CSS `zoom`-centric scaling plus `effectiveZoom` compensation interacts with every FLIP and measurement. Chromium's standardized `zoom` behaviour (gBCR now reflecting zoom) and `currentCSSZoom` are relevant platform facts to verify.

## 10. Build and test toolchain

### Takeaway
The client is built by Webpack 5.111 with `ts-loader` (`transpileOnly`) + `fork-ts-checker` (skippable) + `vue-loader` 17. The main stylesheet is a separate unminified `lessc` step, while SFC `<style>` blocks go through `less-loader → css-loader → style-loader` (runtime injection). Vue is aliased to the full esm-bundler build. Tests are Mocha (server), mochapack + JSDOM (client) and Playwright e2e on a remote Steam Machine. The client webpack build takes roughly 80 s to 4 min.

### Cited Findings
- **Webpack** (`webpack.config.js:80-163`): `devtool: 'source-map'` in all modes; entries `main` + `sw` (`src/client/sw.ts` is an empty service worker: "empty for now, needed for client side notifications"). `ts-loader` with `transpileOnly: true`, `compilerOptions: {module: 'esnext'}` (103-108). `ForkTsCheckerWebpackPlugin` is skipped when `SKIP_TS_CHECK=1` (21-43). `.less`/`.css` → `style-loader` + `css-loader {url:false}` (+ `less-loader`) (110-117). Production gets `CompressionPlugin` gzip + brotli at max quality (65-71). `splitChunks` has a `vendors` catch-all plus `chartjs`/`markdownit` async groups (127-160). `hashFunction: 'xxhash64'`. A separate desktop output `build-desktop` with publicPath `app://bundle/` (`DESKTOP_BUILD=1`, line 19).
- **Vue alias.** `'vue$': 'vue/dist/vue.esm-bundler.js'` (`webpack.config.js:~90`) is the build that includes the runtime template compiler, yet rg finds no `template: '…'` string components in `src/client`. `__VUE_OPTIONS_API__: true`, `__VUE_PROD_DEVTOOLS__: false`.
- **Other configs:** `webpack.embedded.config.js` (70 lines, the embedded server bundle + `scripts/check-embedded-bundle.mjs`) and `webpack.test.config.js` (77 lines, one chunk for mochapack: the "fixed-name `vendors` split silently makes the runner collect ZERO tests" — CLAUDE.md).
- **TS targets.** Root `tsconfig.json:15-27` is `lib: ["dom","es2023"]`, `module: commonjs`, `target: es2021`. `electron/tsconfig.json:11-13` is `commonjs`/`es2021`. TypeScript `^6.0.3`; TS 7 is blocked (memory `typescript-7-blocked.md`). `vue-tsc ^3.3.9` runs as `lint:client`.
- **Less pipeline.** `make:css` = `lessc src/styles/common.less build/styles.css && node src/server/tools/gzip.js`. `common.less` has 131 `@import`s, including the inlined spectre + dialog-polyfill CSS. `watch:less` is `scripts/watchcss.ts`. Only 13 `.vue` files carry a `<style>` block (12 scoped, 10 `lang="less"`); nearly all styling is global Less.
- **Card generation.** `make:cards` = `tsx src/server/tools/export_card_rendering.ts && tsx src/tools/make_card_art_manifest.ts && node scripts/make-card-art-thumbs.mjs` (sharp; incremental thumbs). `make:json` aggregates locales and throws on duplicate keys.
- **Lint.** `eslint.config.mjs` (183 lines, flat config) uses `@eslint/js` recommended, `@typescript-eslint`, `eslint-plugin-vue`, `vue-eslint-parser`; `lint:i18n` = `tsx src/tools/translation_audit.ts`.
- **Tests.** `test:server` = mocha + tsx with a collection floor of 8,500; `test:client` = mochapack + `webpack.test.config.js` + JSDOM with a floor of 4,000, both through `scripts/run-tests.mjs` (`package.json` scripts). Recorded counts: "test:server 12061 / 0; test:client 5711 / 0" (`docs/claude/parliament-sitting-progress.md:1703`). E2E is Playwright `^1.62.1`, run only remotely (`npm run e2e:remote`; CLAUDE.md invariant 14), with a `?fastboot=1&motion=0.5` fast class (commit 301f19b103). Many guard specs enforce CSS/motion rules, e.g. `tests/console/lessDivisionGuard.spec.ts`, `universalSiblingSelectorGuard.spec.ts`, `cursorGrammarGuard.spec.ts`, `transferFlightClock.spec.ts`, `tests/gamepad/glyphLiteralGuard.spec.ts`.
- **Timings (documented).** "`SKIP_TS_CHECK=1 NODE_ENV=production npx webpack` (~80 с)" (memory `ab-baseline-head-client-in-snapshot.md:24`); "full/client webpack build ~1–4 min" (memory `avoid-e2e-on-trivial-cases.md:12`); "`npm run build` + `vue-tsc` in parallel on this machine > 10 min" (memory `turmoil-redux-cloud-development.md:34`). A gotcha: "«Build Finished» with checker errors = bundle NOT rewritten" (memory index `build-client-finished-but-not-emitted.md`).

### Inferences
- Aliasing to `vue.runtime.esm-bundler.js` would drop the template compiler from the bundle (inference; verify that no `template:` option or in-DOM template is used anywhere, including `assets/index.html`).
- `ts-loader` `transpileOnly` + `fork-ts-checker` + `vue-tsc` is a classic, slow-ish setup. A faster transpiler (esbuild-loader/swc-loader) or a bundler switch is a toolchain-modernisation candidate. That is outside "platform features" but relevant to "modern libraries".
- `target: es2021` leaves Chromium 156 downlevelling some syntax unnecessarily (inference: `es2023+` is safe for an Electron-only renderer, though the browser build may have other consumers).

### Gaps
- There are no recorded timings for `make:css` or `vue-tsc` alone, or for the server test suite's wall time.
- I didn't verify whether the browser build (non-Electron) still has users that constrain the TS target or the feature baseline.
