Behind and above the building, over the crater rim, hangs MARS — the whole planet, large in the black sky, rust-red and ochre with a white polar cap and a thin haze at the limb, lit from the side. Only one detail tells the narrative-flipping story, to be found on the second look: at the service gate, a small cargo loader carries shrink-wrapped pallets of freshly printed rally placards into the building — the same placard design in six different party colours (red, green, blue, yellow, purple, white), plain shapes with no readable words.
