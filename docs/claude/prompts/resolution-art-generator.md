## Промт для генератора (один блок, скопировать целиком)

```
You are illustrating a resolution card for "Turmoil Redux", a fan expansion of the board game Terraforming Mars. Attached: (1) a scan of the card — its title, its rule icons and the rule text in parentheses; (2) the emblem of the political party that proposes it. Generate ONE finished illustration for this card right away, without asking questions.

WHAT A RESOLUTION IS. Six parties sit in the Martian parliament and propose laws — resolutions. An enacted resolution changes the economy or the rules for every corporation on Mars. The art is a documentary photograph of that law being carried out, or of the world it creates: Mars in the 2400s, being terraformed by giant corporations under the eye of UNMI, the Earth committee that rates the terraforming effort.

HOW TO READ THE CARD. The TITLE is the main source of the idea. The RULE (icons + the text in parentheses) is a secondary hint: take ONE concrete detail from it, never the whole formula. Icon glossary: yellow rounded octagon with a number = M€, money; black spiky badge with a bust = Influence, a party's weight in parliament; orange square with a laurel globe = Terraform Rating, UNMI's score; orange square with 0100/1001 = data; red square with a smoking man in a suit = corruption; brown square with "?" = underground resource token (excavation beneath the surface); round tags — Mars, Earth, black with a yellow star = space, science, building; green hex = greenery, blue hex = ocean, grey hex = city; mechs, floaters, fighters = resources stored on cards; a red arrow = pay to get; a red strike-through = ignore. IGNORE on the scan: the symbol in the top-left corner (an old tabletop icon — the party is the attached emblem, never draw that symbol), the purple frame and the empty purple grid, the small box in the bottom-right corner (a side goal, not part of the law). If the scan's corner symbol and the attached emblem disagree, the attached emblem wins.

HOW TO FIND THE IDEA — this is where the picture is won or lost. Read the title as a headline in a Martian newspaper and ask which photograph ran under it. The title decides WHAT is happening. The party's lore decides WHO is in the frame, WHERE it happens and in what spirit (proud, defiant, bureaucratic, profit-hungry, earnest). The rule adds one concrete physical detail: what is being carried, counted, sealed, built, seized or shut down. Then, silently, before drawing: (1) draft five genuinely different photographs for this headline — different places, people and moments, and different in KIND: the law at work; the people it hits or the people it helps (every law has a payer and a winner); the moment before it takes effect or the morning after; one telling close-up; a wide aerial or survey view; ordinary life where the law quietly arrives (a hab kitchen, a school corridor, a queue at an airlock, a market stall). (2) Cross out the first idea that came to mind and anything close to THE HOUSE TEMPLATE below — that is what every other card already looks like. (3) Keep the idea most specific to THIS title. Swap test: if the picture could sit under another resolution of the same party, it is too generic. The best frame holds one true-to-life detail that only this law would produce — something just delivered, sealed, repainted, padlocked, queued for or argued over — that the viewer notices on the second look. Calibration, never reuse these scenes or their light: MANUFACTURING RUN — obvious: workers assembling a mech on a factory line; better: a freight yard where forty identical finished mechs stand shrink-wrapped in rows while one inspector walks the line — the run IS the repetition. MARS FIRST BUDGET — obvious: a crane lowering a hab module; better: the first evening in a brand-new neighbourhood under the dome, a family carrying a mattress through a door whose frame is still masked with tape while a neighbour watches from the walkway. Never depict the mechanic literally: no coins, icons, tokens, counters, cards, arrows or numbers. Translate it into real objects: money → contracts, payroll terminals, invoices, sealed cash cases; influence → delegates, petitions, a hearing, a rally, a lobby corridor; data → drives, server racks, sample archives; terraform rating and global parameters → the real hardware of terraforming: heaters, ice haulers, vents, young forests, open water; city / greenery / ocean → real districts, forests, lakes; corruption → bribes, envelopes, off-the-books cargo, shell offices, inspectors; underground tokens → excavation tunnels, drill cores, caverns, deep mines.

THE HOUSE TEMPLATE — earlier illustrations all converged on one frame; do NOT produce it again: a hangar or workshop interior on one side and a big open gate or window onto the orange desert on the other; two to four workers in matching dark coveralls in the party's colours, backs to the camera, handling black hard-shell equipment cases, crates and yellow trolleys; the emblem as a big flat disc on a wall facing the camera; the whole image soaked in warm amber afternoon light; the camera at eye level at a medium distance. Any ONE of these is fine when the idea truly needs it; the combination is forbidden. Equipment cases, crates, trolleys and tablets are set dressing at most, never the subject.

MAKE THE FRAME ITS OWN — after the idea is chosen, choose each of these deliberately and away from the template:
- Place: out on the surface; deep inside with no window to the outside at all; underground (a lava tube, a mine, a cavern); in orbit or on a moon; a civic or domestic interior — a council annex, a clinic, a canteen, a dormitory, a family hab, a classroom, a market.
- Distance and vantage: a close detail of hands and objects; a medium shot; a wide view with small figures; from above (a gantry, a crane cab, a drone); from low down; through a doorway, a helmet visor or a dusty pane.
- Light and time: Martian noon; dawn with white frost on the ground; the blue glow around a small sun at Martian sunset; night under work floodlights or sodium lamps; a dust-storm brown-out; an interior lit only by its own fluorescent, LED or task lamps — neutral or cool, not orange.
- People: one person, a pair, a crowd, or nobody at all — an aftermath can be the strongest frame. Real people of different ages and builds, candid; profiles, three-quarter views and honest expressions (concentration, fatigue, irritation, pride, doubt) are welcome; nobody poses or looks into the lens. Clothing is mixed and specific to who they are: overalls of different companies, municipal vests, lab coats, civilian clothes, patched pressure suits. A party is not an employer — nobody wears the party's colours as a uniform.

THE SIX PARTIES — identify the attached emblem here; the lore sets the spirit of the frame.
- MARS FIRST — emblem: an orange planet Mars with a thin flagpole and an orange flag on top. Mars' populist centrist party: the terraforming is for the Martians first, everyone else second. It invests heavily in the colonists' living standards — housing, clinics, schools, local jobs — at the expense of other parties' projects. Frame: Martian-born colonists, their settlements and neighbourhood infrastructure, local pride; practical and grassroots. Accent: rust orange.
- SCIENTISTS — emblem: a silver-white atom, three elliptical orbits around a glowing nucleus (NOT a flask). A loose collective of rich entrepreneurs who see Mars as the chance to push science to never-before-seen heights and want to be its vanguard, willing to move heaven and earth for it. Frame: privately funded labs, test rigs, observatories, sample vaults, field teams with expensive gear; ambition with money behind it. Accent: silver-white.
- UNITY — emblem: three interlocked bright-blue rings. Sees Mars as one piece of humanity's settlement of the whole solar system; favoured by factions outside Mars, disliked by the Martians themselves, especially by Mars First. Frame: spaceports, orbital docks, interplanetary freight, delegations from Earth, Venus and the Jovian moons, treaty bureaucracy. Accent: blue.
- GREENS — emblem: a lush tree with a rounded crown, bright green to emerald. Haughty environmentalists: the terraforming is humankind's atonement for ruining Earth, so every available resource must go into it; close ties with UNMI. Frame: greenhouses, nurseries, young forests, algae ponds, animals, ecologists; earnest, a little self-righteous. Accent: green.
- REDS — emblem: a red banner with a black clenched fist in its centre. Mars' nationalist conservatives who want the planet to stay as it was; they pursue the outlandish goal of halting the terraforming by enticing companies to do literally anything else; mortal enemies of the Greens. Frame: untouched red desert, preservation, mining and industry instead of terraforming, idle or dismantled terraforming equipment, stubborn crews. Accent: red and black.
- INDUSTRIALISTS — emblem: a golden gear enclosing a factory silhouette with a saw-tooth roof (NOT a globe). The terraforming will go smoother if corporations may profit as much as they want at the start and grow stronger; critics call it profiteering, but Mars is run by corporations, so the party stays popular. Frame: factories, foundries, assembly lines, freight yards, contracts and quotas, corporate efficiency. Accent: gold.

THE EMBLEM IN THE PICTURE. Show the attached emblem exactly once, copied faithfully in shape and colour (a round very dark navy medallion with a thin gold rim and the party's symbol in the centre), on an object that belongs to the story, IN THE DEPTH of the frame: a worn stencil on a vehicle, a container, a tank or a machine in the midground; a pennant or banner seen in perspective; a sticker on a helmet or a toolbox; a small plaque beside a door. It is seen at an angle, lit like everything around it, and may be partly shadowed or partly hidden — the viewer finds it on the second look. Modest size — about one twentieth to one tenth of the image width, never a wall-sized sign. Never a medal or badge in the foreground, never floating, never flat-on to the camera in the middle of a wall, never in the lower-left quarter of the image. The party's accent colour may echo in one or two small places (a pennant, a strip of hazard paint, a cable tag), never as everyone's clothing and never as a colour grade over the whole image.

STYLE — the original Terraforming Mars cards are photographs of real places; match them: photograph or sober engineering visualisation of a real facility, NOT cinematic concept art; standard lens 35–50 mm; asymmetric framing, never a symmetrical vanishing-point corridor; light only from plausible sources; NO volumetric god-rays / glowing haze / lens flares / mirror-polished floors / bloom; muted naturalistic colour, no teal-and-orange grade; hardware utilitarian and USED (cable runs, brackets, scuffs, dust, hazard striping, fasteners); modest human scale — a working facility, not a cathedral; slight grain, no gloss, no AI sheen. Mars is physically plausible: rust-coloured dust on everything that has been outside; a pale butterscotch daytime sky, blue-grey around the sun at sunset; a small sun; outdoors people wear pressure suits and helmets, inside domes and pressurised halls ordinary clothes. Be bold in WHAT you show and sober in HOW you photograph it: originality comes from the idea, the place, the moment and the detail — never from dramatic light, effects or grandeur. The original cards range from a close-up of lichen to a view from orbit: scale and setting change from card to card, the photographic honesty never does.

NEVER: text, letters, numbers, readable signage or any logo other than the party emblem; game icons, tokens, cards, dice or UI; a card frame or border; the purple of the card frame; the tabletop symbol from the card's corner; collage, split screen or inset; heroic posing, glamour portraits, epic vistas; anything large or badge-like in the lower-left corner; the house template described above.

OUTPUT: one landscape image, 1536×1024 (3:2), full bleed. Under the image write one short sentence naming the idea.
```

## Служебное (коротко)

- **Приложить к каждому запуску:** скан карты из `skip` + эмблему партии `assets/parties/redux/<партия>.png`.
  На сканах в левом верхнем углу — СТАРЫЕ настольные значки (у Police Funding там круг со стрелками, а партия —
  Марс вперёд), поэтому эмблему брать по таблице ниже, а не по углу скана.
- **Новый чат на каждую карту** — иначе генератор тянет сцену предыдущей.
- **Выход:** PNG 1536×1024 → `C:\Users\zelin\Downloads\Mars Arts\<Имя карты>_art.png` → импорт при внедрении
  карты: `node scripts/import-card-art.mjs "<png>" RX##` → `npm run make:cards`.
- **Проверить глазами:** эмблема — именно форка (атом, не колба; шестерня с заводом, не глобус), одна, в глубине,
  не в нижнем левом углу; нет текста и игровых иконок; кадр не «AI-открытка» (зеркальный пол, лучи в дымке,
  осевая симметрия) — тогда перегенерить.
- **Проверить на «шаблон дома» (2026-10-20):** пять артов первой волны (Diverted Research, Live Experiments,
  Manufacturing Run, Mars First Budget, Reds Budget) сошлись в ОДИН кадр: ангар слева + ворота в оранжевую пустыню
  справа, 2–4 рабочих в одинаковых комбинезонах цвета партии спиной к камере, чёрные кейсы и жёлтые тележки,
  эмблема большим плоским диском на стене анфас, всё в янтаре, камера на уровне глаз. Причина — сам промт
  («people at work… faces not to camera», «stencil on equipment», акцент «в рабочей одежде», butterscotch-небо в
  каждом кадре) и отсутствие шага выбора между идеями. Сейчас промт велит генератору набросать пять идей,
  отбросить первую и шаблонную, пройти swap-тест и отдельно выбрать место, дистанцию, свет и людей.
  Если в кадре ≥ 3 признака шаблона — перегенерить.
- **Перегенерация в том же чате:** «Another one: a different idea from your list — a different place, distance and
  light, nothing from the previous frame.» Новая карта — по-прежнему новый чат.
- **Стилевой блок не трогать:** креатив добавлен в идею/место/момент/деталь, а фото-честность (35–50 мм, без лучей и
  глянца) — та же; иначе возвращается «AI-открытка».
