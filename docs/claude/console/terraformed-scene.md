## 2. The holds and the ends

- **`terraformed-scene`** — a `registerAnimationHoldSupplier` of the blocking kind with `diagnose` / `expire` over `terraformedSceneUp()`: raised from the ARM (the announce of what follows waits for the planet — PL-660: it once slipped in under the recede), down only while the armed wait is on a surface the player owns (`playerOwned` — a reveal to take), released on the camera's landing (the flow's own completion signal — never a timer of pacing). ## 4. Gotchas (each paid for once)

1. **A poll / WS frame applies the view through `App.update`, not through the transport's `seedRewardHolds`** — seeded on one road only, the scene never saw a rival's last step (the first live run: the HUD flipped, nothing played). Every seeder of this family lives on BOTH roads, in the same synchronous block as the apply.
2. **The ceremony's arc override is declared BEFORE the placement's focus rules at the same specificity** (`.con-board--pfocus … .global-numbers > * {opacity: 0}` at 0,3,0 wins by order) — the arcs were invisible on the focused stage while the sampler read `opacity: 1` on the band. The override is a COMPOUND (`.con-board--pfocus-ceremony.con-board--pfocus`, `…-anim`), never a later copy.
3. **A fixed-height band centred a 6.9rem plate in a 5.65rem box** — 31 px of the claim under the 4K viewport's edge. The band sizes to its plate (`height: auto; min-height; align-items: flex-end; padding-bottom`).
4. **The fixture's only free ocean cell paid two cards** — the API drive answered the lowest free id (06, one card): a reveal stood, the ARMED wait ran its 20 s cap and the scene played over the receded reveal. The brink fixture fills 06 too, so the lowest free cell (07) pays nothing; the e2e walks the cursor onto 13 (two cards) for the «reveal stands» case.
5. **A section displayed and faded in ONE flush pops in whole** — an element that was `display: none` has no before-change style, so an opacity transition started in the same flush as its display never runs: the album came back at full opacity in one frame (the e2e's «came back still faded» sample). The return DISPLAYS the section first (still at 0 under the recede class), waits TWO painted frames (`probeTick` ×2 — a rAF callback runs BEFORE its frame's style recalc, so after one frame the flip would still share the recalc with the display), then drops the recede.
6. **The finale's announce slipped in under the recede** while the ARMED wait held nothing — the armed wait now holds the foreground unless a surface the player owns stands (`playerOwned`); e2e G asserts the plate stands only once the scene is idle (measured 5.4 s after the prompt — the scene's whole phrase; the surrogate held 6.7 s).
7. **A plate UNDER the disc covered the foot of the ocean arc** when the arcs stay on the stage (the «9» marker peeked past the plate's edge) — three mockups over the live focus (`screenshots/tr70/tv4k-mockups/`); the owner chose M2 (2026-10-17): the title is a CAPTION in the zone the player rail vacated, the instrument stays whole.
